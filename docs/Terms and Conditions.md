## 1. Introduction and Acceptance

The Data Collection System ("**DCS**") is the official intake of Megaworld's Risk Assessment & Routing
System ("**RARS**"). It is used to **request, justify, and route approval for access to or collection of
company data**, including personal information protected under the law. By submitting a request through
the DCS, you ("**Requester**") confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these
Terms and Conditions. **If you do not agree, do not submit a request.**

These Terms apply in addition to, and do not replace: Megaworld's Acceptable Use Policy, Information
Security Policy, Data Privacy Policy, the Code of Conduct, and any agreement you sign (NDA, DPA, DSA, or
Confidentiality Undertaking).

## 2. Definitions

- **Personal Information / Sensitive Personal Information**, as defined under the **Data Privacy Act of
  2012 (Republic Act No. 10173, "DPA")** and its IRR.
- **Data Subject**, the individual to whom personal information relates.
- **Processing**, any operation on data: collection, recording, storage, use, sharing, or disposal.
- **ISM**, the Information Security & Management team that oversees RARS and the approval workflow.
- **Approval Ladder**, the sequence of reviewers a request passes through (Immediate Head → Department
  Head → Information Security reviewers → and, where escalated, the Division Head).

## 3. Eligibility and Scope

3.1 The DCS is available only to **authorized users on Megaworld's official domain**, including
employees, accredited agency personnel, and on-the-job trainees (OJTs) acting on legitimate business
need.

3.2 Each request must concern a **specific, lawful, and clearly described business purpose**. Requests
for personal curiosity, personal benefit, or any purpose unrelated to your duties are prohibited.

3.3 Submitting a request does **not** grant access. Access is granted only after the request is
**fully certified** through the Approval Ladder and provisioned by the responsible team.

## 4. Requester Responsibilities and Warranties

By submitting, you warrant that:

4.1 **All information you provide is true, complete, and accurate**, including your identity, your
department, the data requested, the purpose, the accountable persons, and the reporting line (Immediate
Head, Department Head, and where applicable, Division Head) you nominate.

4.2 You will provide **legally valid and properly executed supporting documents** as required for your
request type: Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), Data Protection Agreement (DPA), Data Sharing Agreement
(DSA), Confidentiality Undertaking (CU), and/or an Endorsement Letter for agency personnel or OJTs.
Uploaded files must be genuine, unaltered, and in the accepted formats (PDF, DOC, DOCX).

4.3 You are requesting **only the minimum data necessary** for the stated purpose (data minimization).

4.4 You have the authority to make the request and to bind yourself to these Terms.

4.5 Misrepresenting your identity, your reporting line, or your purpose, including nominating an
approver who is not your legitimate reviewer, is a serious violation.

## 5. Data Privacy Compliance

5.1 All collection and processing of personal data through or following a DCS request must comply with
the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**, its IRR, issuances of the **National Privacy Commission
(NPC)**, and Megaworld's Data Privacy Policy.

5.2 You will uphold the data privacy principles of **transparency, legitimate purpose, and
proportionality**, and respect the rights of data subjects.

5.3 Where your request involves a **Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)** in Phase 2 of RARS, you will
complete it truthfully; the assessment forms part of your request record.

## 6. Confidentiality and Permitted Use

6.1 Any data you are granted access to is **confidential company and/or personal information**. You will
use it **strictly for the approved purpose** and for no other.

6.2 You will **not copy, download, transmit, share, publish, or retain** the data beyond what the
approval permits, and will **not disclose it** to any person not authorized to receive it.

6.3 You will protect the data with appropriate safeguards and will not store it on unauthorized devices,
personal accounts, or unsecured locations.

## 7. Prohibited Uses

You must not, among other things: (a) access data without an approved request; (b) use data for personal,
commercial, or unrelated purposes; (c) re-identify, aggregate, or profile individuals beyond the approved
purpose; (d) circumvent, tamper with, or attempt to bypass the approval workflow, the system's security
controls, or another user's access; or (e) submit false, forged, or misleading information or documents.

## 8. Approval, Routing, and No Guarantee

8.1 Your request is reviewed in sequence by the Approval Ladder. Any reviewer may **approve, return for
correction, escalate, or deny** the request.

8.2 A **"Returned"** request can be corrected and resubmitted; a **"Denied"** request is final for that
submission. Submission does **not** guarantee approval, and approval may be **conditional, time-bound, or
revocable**.

8.3 Decisions, comments, and routing are recorded as part of your request's audit trail.

## 9. Data Handling, Retention, and Disposal

9.1 You will retain accessed data **only for as long as necessary** for the approved purpose and will
**securely dispose of or return** it thereafter, in accordance with company retention rules.

9.2 Draft requests are stored temporarily by the system and **automatically expire**; uploaded files are
stored in a controlled company repository. You are responsible for not embedding sensitive content in
free-text fields beyond what is needed.

## 10. Monitoring, Logging, and Audit

10.1 You acknowledge that **all activity in RARS is logged and may be monitored and audited**,
including submissions, approvals, edits, and access events, for security, compliance, and
investigation purposes.

10.2 The system automatically flags potential integrity risks (for example, conflict-of-interest
patterns). Flagged requests may receive additional scrutiny.

## 11. Accuracy and Updates

You will keep your request information current and will promptly notify ISM if the purpose, scope, or
your authorization changes, or if access is no longer required so it can be revoked.

## 12. Consequences of Violation

Violation of these Terms, the DPA, or company policy may result in **denial or revocation of access,
disciplinary action up to and including termination, and civil or criminal liability** under applicable
law, in accordance with Megaworld's policies and due process.

## 13. Changes to These Terms

Megaworld and the ISM may update these Terms from time to time. The version presented at the time of your
submission governs that request; continued use of the DCS constitutes acceptance of the current version.
